## Closeout

PR: https://github.com/dasch-swiss/sipi/pull/818 (branch fix/libmagic-thread-handle-leak @ 9761d8cf, 14 commits).

### root_cause
`getFileMimetype` cached its loaded libmagic handle in a raw `thread_local magic_t` that was never closed (1f79fa52). `run_with_deadline` (13c4d0e0, DEV-7080) runs every shape probe on a new `std::thread`. Every IIIF image request therefore leaked one handle, about 198 KB by the LSan measurement, and cache hits leaked too, because the shape probe runs before the cache check. The CI sanitizer job had never been able to catch a leak:
- `halt_on_error=0` made LSan non-fatal;
- the unexpanded `log_path=$TEST_UNDECLARED_OUTPUTS_DIR` sent reports where nothing read them;
- in the Rust shell, `crash-handler`'s strong `pthread_create` (via sentry-rust-minidump) outranked ASan's interceptor, so threads were never registered;
- the ASan-inline `run_with_deadline` removed the very thread churn that leaked.

### investigation
- Grafana on prod ruled out allocator retention (retained ~1 GB), decode spikes (budget 0) and wedged threads (0). Growth was monotonic from 0 to 13.3 GB in 72 h.
- A colima replay harness reproduced the leak: 9.1.0 grew 269.6 KB/request, 8.0.0 grew −2.7 KB/request.
- The red test stayed green on CI. Bisecting with canaries isolated `halt_on_error=0`, then the log_path, and then the pthread_create interposition (`nm` plus the linker map).
- Making leaks and UBSan fatal surfaced real bugs:
  - Kakadu `wait_managers` out-of-bounds on hosts with more than 64 cores;
  - PNG and JPEG longjmp skipping destructors;
  - `pct:` float-to-int overflow;
  - the uninitialized ICC `white_point.Y`;
  - EXIF 0/0 becoming NaN in libtiff.
- Kakadu's `array-bounds` finding was verified as its trailing-union idiom.

### solution
- The per-thread handle is an RAII `unique_ptr<magic_set, magic_close>`, and the fixed image replays at 2.5 KB/request.
- The sanitizer job was made genuinely fatal:
  - `ASAN_OPTIONS=detect_leaks=1` only;
  - UBSan halts;
  - e2e judges every spawned server's exit;
  - minidump is left out of ASan builds.
- The four workarounds for the false-positive class are removed.
- Each production bug is fixed at the root in its own commit, ordered before the gate commit that would fail without it.
- User decisions:
  - "everything in this PR";
  - "fix the link and remove the workarounds";
  - Kakadu: verify, then exclude `bounds`;
  - ICC: reject in SIPI (the root cause turned out to be the white point);
  - EXIF: skip 0/0, reject n/0.

### prevention
- The LSan regression test covers 64 short-lived threads.
- Leak and UB canaries are proven RED on the final base. Any leak or UB in unit tests or in e2e-spawned servers now fails CI.
- `.bazelrc` documents why `halt_on_error=0` and `log_path` must never return.
- Open structural gap from the plan: nothing reacts to steady-state RSS approaching the cgroup limit. The admission budget guards decode transients only.
- Post-merge (H1): deploy 9.1.x, then confirm the prod 6 h in-use floor stays under 2 GB for 72 h.
